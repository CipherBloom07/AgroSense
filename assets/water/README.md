# AgroSense Water Intelligence image assets

Place the two optimized, user-supplied Victoria Falls photographs in this folder:

- `victoria-falls-main.webp` — wide hero photograph.
- `victoria-falls-gorge.webp` — gallery photograph.

The Water Intelligence hero includes a Wikimedia Commons fallback until the local hero image is present.

The gallery also links to selected Zimbabwe water resources, including Victoria Falls, Lake Kariba, Lake Chivero, Lake Mutirikwe, Tokwe-Mukosi, Manyuchi Dam, Rusape Dam, Osborne Dam, Mutarazi Falls, Pungwe Falls and Chinhoyi Caves. Each Commons card links to its source page for file-specific licensing and attribution details.

These photographs are illustrative only. They do not represent live satellite imagery, reservoir levels, dam releases or sensor measurements.
