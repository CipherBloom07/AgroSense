How to get a useful result

👁️
Observe closely

Note which leaves are affected and when symptoms started.

🌱
Check soil and roots

Look for dryness, standing water, root damage and compaction.

📷
Keep evidence

Take clear photos and compare healthy and affected plants.

Before you irrigate

Check recent rainfallAvoid unnecessary watering after effective rain.
Check root-zone soil moistureUse field observations or a verified sensor reading.
Confirm crop and growth stageWater demand changes throughout the season.
Inspect for leaks and blocked outletsUneven delivery can waste water and stress crops.

Suggested preparedness actions

🌧️
If heavy rain is forecast

Check drainage, protect stored inputs and postpone non-essential field work where appropriate.

☀️
If hot and dry conditions persist

Monitor soil moisture, review the irrigation schedule and prioritise vulnerable crop stages.

🌬️
If strong winds are expected

Review worker safety and avoid drift-prone spraying conditions.
