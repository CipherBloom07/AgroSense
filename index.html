Module 01 / Crop intelligence

Plant Doctor

Start with what you can observe. Review possible causes and sensible checks before deciding what to do next.

OBSERVATION LAB

Every symptom tells a story.

Capture field observations in a structured way. This rule-based demo is not a confirmed crop diagnosis.

Field check protocol

A useful observation is specific, comparable and recorded.

👁️
1. Observe

Identify affected leaves, pattern, age of symptoms and spread.

🌱
2. Check roots & soil

Look for dryness, standing water, root damage and compaction.

📸
3. Capture evidence

Take photos and compare healthy plants with affected ones.

🧑🏽‍🌾
4. Verify locally

Ask an agricultural extension officer if symptoms spread or crop loss is significant.

Do not apply pesticides or fertiliser based only on this demo. Recommendations need verification against crop, location and qualified agricultural guidance.
Module 02 / Resource optimisation

Water intelligence

Turn field area and irrigation assumptions into a transparent water-volume estimate. This module does not control pumps or valves.

IRRIGATION PLANNER

Every drop should have a purpose.

Estimate net and gross daily volume using a simple, inspectable calculation.

ESTIMATED GROSS DAILY VOLUME156.3 m³/day

Based on 2.5 ha, 5 mm/day and assumed 80% efficiency.

NET WATER APPLIED125 m³/day

Formula: area (ha) × depth (mm/day) × 10 = net m³/day. Gross estimate = net ÷ efficiency fraction.

Before opening the water

Review recent rainfallAccount for effective rain before scheduling irrigation.
Check root-zone moistureUse a verified sensor reading or a field check.
Confirm crop stageCrop demand changes during the growing season.
Inspect delivery systemCheck for leaks, blocked outlets and uneven application.
This calculation is a planning estimate, not a crop-specific prescription. Real irrigation needs depend on crop, soil, weather, rainfall and system design.

Response playbook

Check drainage, protect stored inputs and reassess field access.

Hot and dry conditions

Review soil moisture, vulnerable crop stages and water supply.
