Field check protocol

A useful observation is specific, comparable and recorded.

👁️
1. Observe

Identify affected leaves, pattern, age of symptoms and spread.

🌱
2. Check roots & soil

Look for dryness, standing water, root damage and compaction.

Take photos and compare healthy plants with affected ones.

2. Crop water requirement

Estimate crop evapotranspiration using reference ET and a crop coefficient supplied from a verified agronomic source.

Crop demand analysis
ESTIMATED NET CROP WATER NEED—

Enter ETo, Kc, effective rainfall and field area to calculate an indicative estimate.

Method: ETc = ETo × Kc; indicative net irrigation need = max(0, ETc − effective rainfall). Volume = net depth × area × 10. Use locally validated crop coefficients and account for soil, growth stage and irrigation method. This is not a prescription.
